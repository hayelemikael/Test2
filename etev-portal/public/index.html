Money in and money out (ብር ገቢ እና ወጪ)
Money in = cash deposited + Telebirr + driver payments. Money out = charging + expenses + salaries + overtime + bonus paid.
Net by vehicle (ትርፍ በመኪና)
Money in − money out per vehicle. A day's salary counts on the vehicle the driver had that day.
Fares by source (ገቢ በምንጭ)
What passengers paid, as reported by drivers.
Where the money went (ወጪ በዓይነት)
Expenses spread over their months, plus charging, salaries, overtime and bonus.
Passengers (ተሳፋሪ)
Route passengers from the logbook.
